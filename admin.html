You are signed in as —, but that email is not on the admin list. Every read and write is therefore filtered out by the database, which is why orders look missing and deleting a product appears to work but changes nothing.

Fix it in the Supabase SQL editor, then reload this page:

insert into public.admins (user_id, email, name)
select u.id, u.email, u.raw_user_meta_data ->> 'full_name'
from auth.users u
where lower(u.email) = lower('subhandaraz90@gmail.com')
on conflict (user_id) do nothing;
